# Vendor Management Policy — taskflow

**Owner:** Security Lead · **Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 2026-11-09 · **Next review:** 2027-11-09
**Maps to SOC 2 Common Criteria:** CC9.2

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines how taskflow assesses and manages the security risk of third-party vendors and service providers that store, process or access company or customer data. It applies to all such vendors, including cloud, infrastructure and SaaS providers.

## 2. Vendor inventory

taskflow maintains a list of all vendors that handle company or customer data. For each vendor, the list records: the service provided, the type of data involved, and the vendor's security posture (for example, whether the vendor holds a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report).

## 3. Vendor assessment

- Before onboarding a vendor that will handle sensitive data, its security posture is reviewed. A current SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 report is preferred evidence.
- Vendors are assigned a risk level (low, medium, high) based on the data they handle and their security posture.
- High-risk vendors are reviewed in more depth before approval.

## 4. Ongoing review

- The vendor inventory and the risk level of each vendor are reviewed at least annually.
- A vendor's compliance reports (such as a renewed SOC 2) are collected and kept as evidence.

## 5. Offboarding

When a vendor relationship ends, access is revoked and any company data held by the vendor is returned or securely deleted.

## 6. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Security Lead | Maintains the inventory, assesses and reviews vendors |
| Management | Approves high-risk vendors |

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually by the Security Lead.
