# Information Security Policy — taskflow

**Owner:** Security Lead · **Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 2026-11-09 · **Next review:** 2027-11-09
**Maps to SOC 2 Common Criteria:** CC1.1, CC1.2, CC2.1, CC5.2

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the overall framework for information security at taskflow. It states the company's commitment to protecting customer and company data, and it serves as the parent document for all other security policies.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and services, and to all systems that store or process company and customer data, including the Azure environment, source code repositories, the CI/CD pipeline, and the production application.

## 3. Commitment

taskflow is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the data entrusted to it. Security is treated as a business requirement, not an afterthought, and is considered in design, development and operations.

## 4. Security objectives

- Protect customer and company data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and loss.
- Meet contractual and regulatory obligations, including the path toward SOC 2.
- Detect and respond to security events in a timely manner.
- Build security into the software development lifecycle.

## 5. Governance and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Management | Sponsors the security program and allocates resources |
| Security Lead | Owns the policies, runs reviews, and reports on security posture |
| All staff | Follow the policies and report security concerns |

Security responsibilities are communicated during onboarding, and staff acknowledge the policies in writing.

## 6. Supporting policies

This policy is implemented through the following documents:

- **Access Control Policy** — how access is granted, reviewed and removed.
- **Incident Response Policy** — how security incidents are detected and handled.
- **Change Management Policy** — how changes reach production safely.
- **Vendor Management Policy** — how third-party risk is managed.
- **Data Classification Policy** — how data is classified and protected.

## 7. Risk management

taskflow maintains a risk assessment that identifies risks to its objectives, rates them by likelihood and impact, and tracks their treatment in a risk register. The assessment is reviewed at least annually.

## 8. Compliance and enforcement

- Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Violations may result in disciplinary action.
- The security posture and the effectiveness of controls are reviewed periodically, and findings are tracked to resolution.

## 9. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or after a significant change to the business or environment, by the Security Lead, with management approval.
